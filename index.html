Explore your business fit
01Are we selling our original records?

The source opportunity is presented as licensing exported copies, with assistance during the process. Your original records and working systems stay with you. The actual agreement must define ownership, permitted uses, exclusivity, term, and any refresh rights—review those terms before deciding.

02Which companies may qualify?

Cloudwrx’s general guidance is businesses in the United States or Canada with at least three years of history and 20 or more people at a desk. Headcount, including past scale, record volume, systems, rights, and quality affect the review. These are starting criteria, not automatic approval; HistoryPays and the licensing provider determine fit.

03Is the estimated value a guaranteed quote?

No. The public program range, provider-reported examples, and calculator are educational reference points—not a quote, offer, typical result, or payment promise. The buyer model uses simplified assumptions. Actual value depends on dataset quality, coverage, rights, relevance, and agreed terms.

04What happens to private or identifying information?

The provider describes replacing names and other identifying information before use. Start with a high-level fit check—not a raw-data upload. Ask what will be removed, how the result is reviewed, what information is excluded, who can access it, and what the contract permits. De-identification is a process to verify, not a blanket guarantee that all privacy risks disappear.

05When might we receive payment?

The source says payment is usually 90–120 days after signing, but nobody can promise a date. Qualification, review, exports, agreed terms, and completion affect timing. Beginning a fit check or seeing a calculator estimate does not establish an immediate payout.

06Can a future data refresh create more value?

The source calculator shows a possible refresh within 24 months. Treat that only as an optional illustration. A new licensing or refresh payment depends on fresh useful records, buyer interest, rights, and a new or existing agreement; recurring income and a future deal are not guaranteed.

07What information should I share first?

Begin with your industry, approximate team size, years of records, and the systems you use. The short fit check is handled by HistoryPays. Do not submit passwords, private client files, tax returns, personal details from your records, or raw datasets through this page.
